HOW TO SWAP IN YOUR OWN PHOTOGRAPHY
  Every artwork is a <div class="art ..."> holding a gradient placeholder
  (<div class="surface">) plus, for real photos, an <img class="photo">:

     <div class="art iri"><div class="surface"></div>
       <img class="photo" src="img/your-photo.jpg" alt="describe it">
       <span class="cap">your caption</span></div>

  Photos live in img/. If the <img> is missing, the holographic surface
  gradient shows instead — that's the intentional placeholder look.
  The soft-focus/film overlay sits on top automatically, so photos inherit
  the dreamy Petra-Collins wash. Remove ".art img.photo{filter:...}" in the
  <style> if you want them perfectly sharp.
